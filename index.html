What is moringa (murungai) powder?
Moringa oleifera — known as murungai in Tamil — is called the "miracle tree." The leaves are dried and ground into a fine green powder packed with over 90 nutrients, 46 antioxidants, and all 9 essential amino acids. Moringai's powder is cold-processed at low temperatures to preserve maximum nutrition.
What are the health benefits of moringa powder?
Moringa leaves are naturally rich in vitamin C, calcium, potassium, iron and plant protein, which is part of why it's called a superfood. It's a nutritious addition to a balanced diet — not a treatment for any medical condition, and not a substitute for medication or professional advice.
How do I use moringa powder daily?
Start with ½ teaspoon (2g) and build up to 1–2 teaspoons per day. Mix into smoothies, warm water with lemon, coconut milk, dal, chutneys or rasam. Avoid adding to boiling liquids — heat above 60°C reduces nutrient density.

Is moringa powder safe? Are there any side effects?
Moringa is generally well tolerated by most adults in food quantities. Pregnant women should avoid moringa root or bark extracts, and should check with a doctor before adding leaf powder to their diet. Start with a small amount if you have a sensitive stomach. If you take blood pressure or diabetes medication, speak to your doctor before regular use, as moringa may interact with these medicines.
How long does it take to notice a difference?
There's no fixed timeline, and we don't promise specific results — moringa is a food ingredient, not a supplement with guaranteed effects. Most people simply make it part of their daily routine, the way it has been used in Tamil Nadu kitchens for generations.
