Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Our 30-day return promise

If you're not happy with your order, you can request a return within 30 days of delivery.

How to request a return

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 95144 99924 with your Order ID and the reason for return. We'll confirm the next steps with you directly.

Condition for returns

Because Moringai sells packaged food products, opened or used items may not always be eligible for return on hygiene and safety grounds — please contact us first and we'll let you know what applies to your specific order.

Refunds

Once a return is approved and received (where applicable), refunds for online payments are issued to your original payment method via Razorpay. For Cash on Delivery orders, we'll confirm a refund method with you directly.

A confirmed refund processing timeline (in business days) will be added here once supplied by the Moringai team. See MORINGAI_OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED.md.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item — we'll make it right.

Order cancellations

You can request cancellation before an order is dispatched by messaging us on WhatsApp with your Order ID.
